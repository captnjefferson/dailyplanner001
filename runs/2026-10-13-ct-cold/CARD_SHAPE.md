# Card shape (cold send, Jefferson's voice)

- Subject: specific to the angle, 4 to 8 words, no "AI" alone, no "quick question".
- Line 1: the angle. Something only this person could receive, from their own words or room. One sentence.
- Lines 2 to 3: what he delivers, from the Andy Cobb reply, trimmed to what fits the room: "Leading an AI-Ready Organization" (keynote or 60–90 min, execs/boards), "PromptLab" (90 min or half day, hands-on, people work on their own real tasks), "Concepts You Need to Know to Work with Today's AI" (60 min). Two IHA sessions last week; GHA repeat. T3 cards skip the catalog and offer a conversation.
- Line 4: one ask. A 20-minute call; or, when the pays grade is C, "Do you bring in outside speakers, and do you pay them?"
- Close: "Thanks much!" or "Let me know." then the standard signature.
- Voice: warm, direct, contractions, a dash or two, no bullets, no attachments, under 140 words. Humor allowed when the angle earns it.
- Never: "I hope this finds you well," "I'd love to pick your brain," any claim not in a research file.
