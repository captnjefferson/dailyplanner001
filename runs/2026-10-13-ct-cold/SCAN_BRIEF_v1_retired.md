# Per-target scan brief (the subagent prompt, filled per person)

Read-only. Never contact anyone, never log in, never visit LinkedIn (the lead does LinkedIn in its own tab). WebSearch, WebFetch, curl only. Every claim carries a source URL or says "not found". Mark anything not confirmed on the primary page "unverified". Never invent.

**Target:** {name} · {org} · {role} · thread {T1|T2|T3} · seed: {candidate file + section}

**Produce** `{thread}/{slug}.md` with these sections:

1. **Identity (layer 1).** Two anchors that prove this is the same person (org + role, org + city, a photo caption, a bio line). Label: VERIFIED-PRIMARY / SEARCH-ONLY / UNVERIFIED. If a same-name collision exists, say so and how it was ruled out.
2. **Publishing map (layer 2).** Where they put words: personal site, newsletter, podcast (host or guest), blog, X, YouTube, press quotes. Up to 8 URLs, each with a one-line note and date of the latest item.
3. **Consumption and register (layer 3).** What they read, follow, cite, recommend, belong to: newsletters they mention, podcasts they've guested on or praised, books they cite, communities and associations, the vocabulary they use. This is where the angle usually comes from. Up to 8 items with sources.
4. **Recent (last 90 days).** The 3 to 5 most recent public items, dated, one line each, with any AI mention quoted verbatim.
5. **Room facts.** T1: chapter size, recent learning events (AI-themed?), how speakers get booked, honorarium language. T2: the event series, who presented last, paid-speaker evidence, grade A/B/C/D with source. T3: company size evidence, founder-led confirmation, the AI statement verbatim with URL and date.
6. **Handles.** Any consistent username across platforms (for Maigret, run by the lead).
7. **Angle candidates.** Three one-line openings only this person could receive, each pointing at the source item (section + URL). No pitch language.
8. **Sources.** Every URL used.

Bounds: 40 minutes. Partial is fine; say what was blocked.
