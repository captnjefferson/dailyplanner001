**Terms.** Vistage is a paid CEO peer-advisory network. A chair runs a group of owners that meets monthly for a full day, coaches each member one to one, and books the outside speaker. A "CE" group is Vistage's chief-executive group; Chair Academy is Vistage's training for new chairs. "Issue processing" is the group's method: one member brings a problem and the room questions it. A business operating system (BOS) is a set of meetings, scorecards and roles for running a company; EOS is the best-known one. Ninety (ninety.io) is software for running a BOS. BOS-UP (bos-up.coach) trains coaches to install a BOS on Ninety. Predictive Index is a workplace personality assessment. TM is Transcendental Meditation, a mantra meditation. *Confessions of a Vistage Chair* is Ryan Hogan's podcast interviewing chairs (Hogan is CEO of Talent Harbor, a sales-recruiting firm). *The Ambitious Coach Lab* (ACL below) is Dr. Cam Lawson's podcast for business coaches, sponsored by Ninety.
