## 2. Who he is

David Case started Advastar in 2012 "after working for a large global staffing firm and recognizing the opportunity to build a more responsive, relationship-focused business" (chapter bio). The firm places engineers, construction leaders and skilled trades nationwide and runs "a satellite office in Vietnam to deliver cost-effective back-office and technical resources." He also "leads Walnut Creek Investments, a real estate investment company focused on residential and short-term rental properties across the Kansas City metro" (https://www.eokansascity.com/david). He has been in EO for thirteen years and has held three chapter seats.

His bylined posts make the people side the point: "Every day in my job, I'm reminded that construction is ultimately a people business. The cranes, concrete, and steel don't come together by themselves." He describes calling tradespeople a week after placing them, and says "I regularly speak at a nearby vocational institute that trains electricians and HVAC techs. It's not a hard sell pitch; I share real stories from job sites" (2025-07-26). On the stakes for clients: "It was barely dawn when I answered a panicked call from a real estate developer… As the head of a construction recruiting firm, I've come to expect these 7 AM SOS calls" (2025-07-16).
