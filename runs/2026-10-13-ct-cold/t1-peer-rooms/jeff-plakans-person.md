## 2. Who he is

He grew up inside payroll. His father, "a CPA… in finance," built Payrolls Plus in the early 1980s, and "my brother and I were free labor… stuffing W-2s by hand into envelopes." His brother ran operations and he ran sales until the family sold the company in 2005, when he was in his mid-thirties. He then worked 11 months for a larger company: "I was the most miserable prick on the earth. And my wife will stand as the primary witness to that." He spent three months planning Commonwealth. "I'm an entrepreneur because nothing's ever set up to my satisfaction."

His view of the work is close to vocation: "it's almost a higher calling… like the relationship between a doctor and a patient… that's how sacred the relationship that we have with our clients is." He tells his kids, "you may work for somebody else but really what you're doing is you're practicing to work for yourself."

On family, on record (2022): "when my kids were young i was a very passionate coach… my daughter ended up as a ncaa softball player my son is a current ncaa football player… my wife [and I] both live vicariously through our children." He lives on the North Shore of Massachusetts, near the water, "so we spend time on our boats." In 2026 he said his daughter "works in HR for… Boston Red Sox." No faith or health context found.

Sources: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLNNO6ngF5o (minutes 1 to 9, 30 to 32) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYuotaBw6Cc (minutes 1, 41 to 42). Quotes come from auto-captions with light cleanup.
