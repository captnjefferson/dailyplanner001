## 2. Who he is

Michael McCarty founded EDGE Fall Protection in 2017. His bio says he brings "more than a decade of hands-on fall protection experience … installing, selling, designing, and engineering fall protection systems." It lists Competent Person and Competent Person Trainer credentials, "First Responder experience, Firefighter I certification, Hazmat Operations," and "more than 228 hours of documented fire department training." The company calls him "a lifelong learner, problem-solver, and explorer" who "has spent several cumulative months in alpine environments." He holds California and Arkansas contractor licenses for the firm. He went to Saint John's University and joined EO in 2019.

His own words are few. On the chapter page: "You don't have to learn it the hard way - someone has already done that!" (undated). The tone is plainspoken and field-first.
