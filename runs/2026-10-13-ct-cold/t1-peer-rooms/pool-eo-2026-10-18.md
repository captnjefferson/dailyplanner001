# T1 pool: EO learning chairs, verification pass (2026-10-18)

**Scope.** This file covers twelve candidates from `t1-candidates.md` (entries 2 to 8, 10 to 14). Luther (1), Dixon (9) and the Vistage and TAB entries are covered elsewhere.

**Terms.** EO is the Entrepreneurs' Organization, a peer network of business owners. Each chapter is a local nonprofit with a volunteer board. The learning chair books the chapter's speakers and runs its learning events. Boards turn over each July 1, so every role has to be confirmed for the 2026-27 board year. A SAP (Strategic Alliance Partner) is a paying chapter sponsor. "Learning Day" and "Accelerator Masterclass" events follow the Scaling Up framework (Cash, Strategy, People, Execution).

**Method.** All fetches were made with curl (desktop User-Agent) on 2026-10-18. Sources:
- every chapter board page;
- the public EO event feed (`member.eonetwork.org/<chapter>/upcoming-events`, pages 1 to 5);
- the public EO chapter stats block (`member.eonetwork.org/<chapter>/`), which gives membership, median sales and employees;
- Wayback Machine snapshots of board pages that carry no year;
- each chapter's latest IRS Form 990, as rendered by ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer;
- company sites.

WebSearch was used about 25 times, only where those sources ran out. Pages not followed:
- EO event detail pages redirect to a member login.
- The Leading Authorities and Keppler bureau pages returned a bot wall (403 and "Client Challenge").

No LinkedIn page was opened, and no one was contacted.

**How the role year was settled.**
- A page headed "2026-2027" counts as current.
- A page with no year counts as current only if both of these hold: (1) its roster has turned over since a Wayback snapshot taken before July 1, 2026, for example last year's president-elect is now president; and (2) the candidate still holds the role on the new roster.
- Anything else is marked "not confirmed".

**Learning budgets.** The 990 figures below come from each chapter's own IRS filing for the fiscal year ending June 2025, the latest public (Long Island files on the calendar year; its figure is 2024). They show how much the room spends on learning. None of the lines is labeled "speaker fee" or "honorarium", so none earns grade A alone.
