## 2. Who he is

He was raised to pick between "Doctor or an attorney," and he wanted trial law: "Harvey, how he is in court, that was my style." He quit before law school: "I made the dreadful call to my mom… She was crying because I let her down." Always "the kid selling gum to other kids," he hated commercial real estate. Then came 2008–09: "Career gone, all my money gone… I'm gathering all of that up and taking it to the grocery store, put in the coin star machine so I can buy groceries." He gave himself "four years" to make aquariums viable. With "my business partner" he built filtration that worked, and he learned to price it: "I raised it by 47%." When Georgia offered film tax breaks he sold set work ("Black Panther… his spaceship"), "just opportunity," since wound down (*THIS is a Thing*, 2026-07-02).

On himself: "I have a very huge fatal flaw that no matter what it is, I'm going down that rabbit hole… If I'm going in, it's deep end all the way," and "I was always the risk taker. I always swung for those fences." Family is the motive he names; per UGA, "the excitement he gets from his three sons sits at the top." "Seeing their eyes beaming with joy… has kept me in the cool category." His rule for projects is "cool, fun, and sexy." He bought Reef Builders to "uphold Jake's legacy," meaning the late co-owner Jake Adams. Faith and health: not found on record.

**INFERENCE (two signals): he is at home on both sides of a stage, as speaker and as booker.** (1) He "has been guest speaker at various aquarium and entrepreneur conferences around the U.S." (2023 release). (2) He co-hosts the Reef Therapy podcast and owns the ReefStock expos, which book speakers (reefstock.show).
