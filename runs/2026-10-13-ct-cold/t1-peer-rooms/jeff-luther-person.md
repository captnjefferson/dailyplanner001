## 2. The person read

**Who he is.** Jeff Luther grew up in a Georgia town of about 1,700, the middle of three boys, in a family he calls "purple collar" [GHF 5:26]. He cut grass as a kid and left college, then technical school, then community college: "I just wanted to go earn money" [GHF 11:00]. After two years at Georgia-Pacific he married, bought a house and quit the job in the same week, and started Home-Probe in 2002 [GHF 18:37]. His first goal was $50 a day, tracked on a calendar while he worked at Home Depot for the health insurance [7F]. He grew it past "3 million" and 30 employees, and none of it felt like enough: "the hedonic treadmill" [DM]. In June and July 2021 his heart stopped twice, and in August his marriage ended. He tells the rebuild as quitting, then agreeing to "30 seconds" more [GHF 56:17–59:07]. He names his failures plainly: "I quit on my kids. I quit on me" [GHF].
