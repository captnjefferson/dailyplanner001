# T1 peer rooms: candidate speaker bookers (CEO peer groups, US)

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Method:** WebSearch, WebFetch, curl only. No logins, no contact, no LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram/X pages opened.
- **What I searched:**
  - EO chapter board pages: about 90 US chapter domains probed; about 25 live board pages read.
  - EO public chapter event feeds: `member.eonetwork.org/<chapter>/upcoming-events`, pages 1 to 4, for 18 chapters.
  - TAB local facilitator microsites: 86 pages, found through the thealternativeboard.com sitemap.
  - Vistage regional chair pages, plus the Vistage and Vistage Florida speaker pages.
  - The "Confessions of a Vistage Chair" podcast feed (2026 episodes).
- **What was blocked or thin:**
  - **Vistage:** the national chair directory is not publicly searchable. Chair pages do exist on regional licensee sites (for example florida.vistage.com/vistage-chair/<name>/). Four of the five Vistage people below are confirmed only by a podcast page or an event page, so their role is marked unverified.
  - **TAB:** the "find a local board" facilitator search loads by script and returned nothing. I used the local microsites from the sitemap instead.
  - **EO global:** the chapter-leadership page (eonetwork.org/one/chapter-leadership/) redirects to a login, so I did not follow it.
  - **EO chapter sites:** eobaltimore.org returned 403. eonj.org is a parked domain. member.eonetwork.org/longisland/upcoming-events returned 404.
  - **Web search:** it returned nothing useful for most EO learning chairs by name. Their public footprint is mostly "not found" beyond their company site.
- **Result:** 20 people profiled (14 EO, 5 Vistage, 1 TAB), plus a short list of other leads at the end.
- **Booking rules found:**
  - **Vistage (strongest):** outside speakers are onboarded only if a Chair has already set a meeting date with them, or if they are already in the Vistage community. So the chair is the door.
  - **EO:** no public "speak at our chapter" page, application form or honorarium language turned up for any chapter. The learning chair runs the calendar.
  - **No pay language:** I found no honorarium or fee language anywhere.
