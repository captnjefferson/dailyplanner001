## 2. Who he is

A father, "one of my daughters went to Yale"; his daughters live in "Brooklyn and DC and Boston" and grew up in Menlo Park. He refers on record to "my now ex." His Substack bio: "Bay Area runner, dad, CEO coach." Health and balance drive him.
