I love to persuade." He talks in three points and a joke, scripture and golf and a Terminator line in one answer, and says "I don't know" often. He shut down for two and a half weeks "and it hasn't hurt the business," and the succession work he sells to churches turned out to be his own insurance: "I need to be a little less relevant every year around here so the thing can live after me." His stated regret: "I probably wouldn't have traveled as much." Faith shows up as text he knows cold, not piety: Job's friends sat silent for seven days, so "I should have talked less." Dave Ramsey is his North Star on money, peace of mind over scale. Right now: 19 of 38 names gone from the team page in a year, four new Director of Consulting titles, an Operations and Technology Manager posting that moves the tech stack off his desk, his own podcast quiet while the COO's show launched a second season, Glassdoor at 2.9 with two 2026 reviews saying "constant change," succession openly on the table ("Hiring a CEO? Very possible"), a new book in January. His named problem: agents that do the work "and still guard what I consider to be very private information," and bringing along staff "still using a legal pad… I'm just not much help there." He is in a five-figure AI owners' collective and subscribes to Salesforce's agents newsletter. Humantic CDI "The Enigma": hard to convince, friendly yet blunt, 100 to 120 words, no salutation, proof and detail, charmed by energy, not by the relationship. The open slot is the founder-and-team handoff, not an AI talk.

He quit. A coach asked for 30 seconds of kettlebell swings. "That's where I got my life back, 30 seconds at a time." The coaching practice is that story. All three sons were tested; Cash saw it happen; the family now "go into the danger when we have a difficult conversation." He consumes the speaker trade: the NSA's blog, a speaker agency, podcast-monetization letters, a cardiologist keynote speaker. His comments are "So inspiring!" and "Y'all have 20!!!??" Humantic DIS "The Pioneer," strong fit: first name, informal and direct, 100 to 120 words, one exciting idea, warmth and social proof work. Meet him speaker to speaker.
