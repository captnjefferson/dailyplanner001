# T2 widen: sponsored rooms, 2026-10-20

**What this is.** Four new T2 candidates (sponsored rooms: a regional professional-services firm that hosts recurring events for business owners and brings in outside speakers). Each passes the new email gate first: the convener's own address is printed somewhere public. Each then passes the verification standard in `EXPERIMENT_PLAN_2026-10-18.md` section 6: identity and current role on a primary source, the room with a recent or upcoming date, and a pay grade with its source. Nobody in `POOL_2026-10-18.md`, `t2-sponsored-rooms/pool-2026-10-18.md` or `t2-sponsored-rooms/t2-candidates.md` is repeated, and no firm checked there is reused.

**Method.** Read-only, 2026-10-20, about 07:25 to 10:30 ET. Tools: curl on raw HTML (mailto links, JSON-LD, Cloudflare `data-cfemail` decoded by XOR with the first byte), `pdftotext` on PDFs, WebFetch, WebSearch (about 45 queries, low yield on this topic), the Wayback Machine, and podcast RSS feeds. Two sweeps by machine: the FDIC list of active banks with $0.7B to $15B in assets (1,231 banks, 1,224 sites; home pages plus about 2,100 events, seminar and education pages), and the events pages of about 100 regional CPA firms and 60 law firms. Two helper agents covered insurance brokers and PEOs (a PEO is a company that runs HR, payroll and benefits for small employers), and CPA firms and wealth advisors. Every load-bearing fact from a helper was re-fetched by the lead before it went in this file. No logins, no forms, no contact with anyone, no Chrome, no address guessed from a pattern, no data-broker listing used (Wiza showed up in one search and was ignored). Blocked and not crossed: quadcitiesbusiness.com (403), allamericanspeakers.com (Cloudflare), leadingauthorities.com (bot check).

**Grade key.** A = a past speaker in the room is listed by a speakers bureau with a fee, or there is an honorarium line. B = a named outside keynote or presenter, or sponsor-funded. C = unknown. D = pay-to-play (excluded).
