Research task, read-only. Do not contact anyone.

Find 25 founders or founder-CEOs of US companies with roughly 25 to 100 employees who, in the last six months, said something in public about their own uncertainty, learning curve, or open questions about using AI in their business. Sources that count: a LinkedIn post, a newsletter issue, a blog post, a podcast appearance, an X post, a local-press interview. Founder-led only: the founder still runs the company. Any industry.

For each person give:
1. Full name
2. Company, and one line on what it does
3. Employee count, with the source (LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase, ZoomInfo, a press mention, the company site)
4. The post or episode: URL, date, and a 1 to 2 sentence verbatim quote that shows the uncertainty or the question
5. Where they publish: newsletter, podcast, blog, X handle, LinkedIn URL
6. City and state

Prefer people who publish regularly (a newsletter, a podcast, steady LinkedIn posting). Exclude AI companies, agencies that sell AI services, VC-backed startups under three years old, and anyone whose post is promotional rather than reflective.

Output: a table with the six columns above, then a list of every source URL used. Mark anything you could not verify as "unverified". Do not invent names, quotes, or counts; write "not found" instead.
