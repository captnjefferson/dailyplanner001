## 2. Who he is

Tim Mages was born in Buffalo and "spent the bulk of my career in Atlanta," first as an investment banker and then "about five or six years at various debt funds," SBIC funds (debt funds licensed under the Small Business Administration's investment-company program). He watched that program drift upmarket: "You can't deploy $200,000. You got to deploy $5 million... they kind of left small businesses behind." In July 2014 his fund let him go. The next day a former client he hadn't spoken to in two years called about a Sioux Falls lender he had invested in: "it was kind of God's way, I guess, of maybe getting me to Sioux Falls."

His motive is the owner and the jobs: "I would never bet against the American small business owner. They're some of the most tenacious... most creative people in this country." Small businesses drive "60 to 65% of the employment growth... which ultimately then is helping families." COVID cut ECG's portfolio "down 70%." He calls the forced pause "one of the blessings of COVID," because it pushed the firm to automate until "90% of our approvals go out within 45 minutes."

On culture he cites Peter Drucker ("culture eats strategy for breakfast," attribution his, hedged) and tells a story about Herk Christie keeping a dance bet: "you're only as good as your word." On community: "it's about the present success of our Sioux Falls community" (2026-04-01). Family and health: not found on record.
