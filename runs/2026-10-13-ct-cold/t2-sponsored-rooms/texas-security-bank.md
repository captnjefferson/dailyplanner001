## 3. Room facts (T2)

### Format, cadence, audience
- **Cadence:** monthly, usually the last Tuesday. Summer break (June or July–August); 2026 ran Jan–Jun 2, then Sep–Nov. 2025: 8 sessions. 2026: 8 sessions.
- **Format history:**
  - 2015–2016: "TSB Business Institute," 10 free classes a year (2015 and 2016 reports).
  - 2017–2019: in-person lunch at the Farmers Branch training center, 11:30–1:00. Registration asked "Invited By," and full sessions had waitlists (Oct 29, 2019 page: "Oops - class full!").
  - 2020–2021: a second track, "BOLD Speaker Series 2.0," 8:00–9:30 a.m., with the Garland Chamber.
  - From 2020: Zoom webinars, 12:00–1:30.
  - From late 2024: 12:00–1:00 webinars, plus occasional in-person events at TIB (Nov 19, 2024, 12:00–1:30; Oct 27, 2026, 8:30–noon, the first half-day "summit").
- **Cost to attend:** free. The 2026 registration form asks only first name, last name, email, and "Gross Annual Revenue: Under $1MM in GAR / Over $1MM in GAR." No payment field. 2019 included a complimentary lunch.
- **Audience:** "business owners and key leaders" of owner-managed businesses; a bank relationship is not required for the Academy (FAQ). The revenue question shows the bank sorts attendees at $1MM.
- **Scale:**
  - 2025: "869 Business Speaker Series attendees," "902 Business owners educated," "23 Business classes," "33 TSB Academy graduates." 869 ÷ 8 sessions ≈ 109 a session (arithmetic; per-session counts not published).
  - 2023: "We helped 670 business owners build better businesses," "26 classes," "24 TSB Academy graduates."
  - 2017: "More than 4,500 people have participated to date."
  - Sources: 2025 report (above), 2023 report https://www.texassecuritybank.com/media/glghzn1s/2023-tsb-annualreport-final-online.pdf, 2017 report (above).
- **Awards:** Texas Bankers Association LiFE (Leaders in Financial Education) 2021, for "our TSB Academy, Business Speaker Series and BOLD Speaker Series 2.0" (2021 report); TBA Cornerstone Award 2022, accepted by McQuade (news page, 6/6/2022).
- **Recordings:** some posted to Vimeo and the resource center (2020–2022; Mar 2025 cash-flow session). Plays are low (1–68 each; public Vimeo feed).
