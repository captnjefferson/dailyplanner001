## 2. Who she is

Angela Dotson grew up in Statham, Georgia, "about 20 minutes from Athens," the daughter of a UGA alum and "the child of entrepreneurs" (GSCPA). The family business is the root of her work: she was "actively involved in the day-to-day operations" and "witnessed firsthand the stress her parents experienced due to working with an unreliable CPA" (Aprio, 2025-07-28). "I had a desire to do something with a career that I could tangibly see and know how I had helped." She interned at Tauber & Balser, stayed through its 2008 merger into what became Aprio, and names Bob Greenberger as her mentor (GSCPA). The Atlanta Business Chronicle named her a 40 Under 40 in 2012; she is a Leadership Atlanta graduate (Class of 2018; a year-long civic leadership program).

Her community work leans toward women's health and youth: past treasurer of TurningPoint Women's Healthcare ("a specialized breast cancer rehab facility"), Komen Atlanta Sisters of Promise Advisory Council, and board member of C5 Georgia ("a five-year college readiness program for challenged youth"). Her bio says she "empowers women by educating them on tax planning."

On the Forum: "My parents were entrepreneurs. So… small to medium sized businesses have… a huge place… in my heart" (2024-07-17). "Based on what I see in my role and what I saw in my family, I know the areas of struggle and where there's a financial literacy gap… We're here to let Black business owners know that there are people who understand where they're coming from" (2025-07-28). On her team: "we have a phrase that we use and it's two feet in."
