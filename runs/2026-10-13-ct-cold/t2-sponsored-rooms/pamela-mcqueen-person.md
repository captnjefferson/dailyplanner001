## 2. The person read

Pamela McQueen has run Texas Security Bank's business education for at least six years and its marketing and branding for at least five. She was published as Pamela McQuade until late 2024. The bank, founded in Dallas in 2008 and now over $1 billion in assets, sells itself to owner-managed companies on one idea: it teaches them to run better businesses. She is the person who makes that true. She "is responsible for all aspects of both the TSB Academy and the TSB Speaker Series." The Academy is a year-long $2,500 course of 15 classes (2026 curriculum). The Speaker Series is a monthly session with an outside speaker that drew 869 attendees in 2025. She is also the bank's press contact ("Marketing Director Contact," Jan 2024). She accepted the Texas Bankers Association's Cornerstone Award for the programs in 2022. D CEO picked her as a mentor in its first Emerging Women Leadership Network (2023–24).

Her path, as she has told it, is not a straight banking career. In a 2014 essay on her own site she wrote that it "started as a small town girl from Iowa and fluctuated between various cycles of career success and failures, punctuated by an identity crisis along the way… retail management, V.P. of a financial institution, a spiritual quest and awakening, authoring an International Award Winning book… becoming a wife, a mother of two boys, discovering my intuitive and healing gifts and becoming an entrepreneur." Her 2010 bio says she was raised "in a staunch Catholic family," lived "in a destructive relationship," and "was a banker in the midst of financial devastation" before she "quit her long held corporate career and spent half a decade following the call of her heart and soul." In those years she was a Reiki Master, "a Reverend with the Universal Life Church," and the author of *Spiritually Rich and Sexy* (2011). She also made a meditation album (2010) and ran healing retreats. On motherhood she wrote that it "was neither, natural nor easy for me." She wrote that her "older son was also diagnosed at age four with Aspergers." She described "the 'can't get off the couch'… type of depression," and said she came out of it through "soul searching, faith, healing and what I can only describe as miracles." All of this is on her own pages, 2010–2014, under the McQuade name. The tie to the banker rests on the signals in section 1.

The banking-era story she tells now is shorter and more polished. *Texas Banking* (Jan/Feb 2025) opens with her turning down Herb Kelleher, who told her, "You should come work for me," in favor of banking, where "she could make a more direct impact on small businesses and entrepreneurs." The magazine calls her role with the Academy "one of McQueen's proudest achievements." She quotes Zig Ziglar ("You cannot consistently perform in a manner that is inconsistent with the way you see yourself") and says, "I am always stretching, always growing, always challenging myself."

She returns to four themes. **Owners were never taught to run a business:** "There is a misperception that if you've started a business you know how to run a business" (2021). **Marketing is the client's story, not the product:** "The focus is always the client and how you can best serve their unique needs" (2021); "it's about crafting a story that resonates with clients' goals and values" (2025). **The founders' risk is the brand:** "they have lived what it is like to try to make payroll and navigate tough times… This is a story that connects with people's hearts" (2021). **Women leading:** "I'm honored to work alongside such an impressive group of female leaders" (2022), plus the D CEO mentorship. What she praises: founders who took the risk, women who "implemented many successful initiatives," and growth. What she mocks: nothing found in the bank era.

Signals: (1) the bank's own copy presents speakers as "TSB Academy Instructors" (Apr 2024, Mar 2025); (2) repeat speakers across years (Jamie Crosbie 2019, 2020 and 2025; Mike Sharrow 2022 and 2026; Value Catalyst twice in 2026; ITR Economics in most Januaries); (3) the Oct 27, 2026 slot was "TBD" in March and was filled from the April speakers.
