## 2. Room facts (T2)

**What it is.** Free in-person gatherings for Black business owners and executives, launched 2023, "typically quarterly" in 2024 (Dotson), two or three a year since. Format: 45 to 60 minutes networking, about an hour of panel content, then networking. Audience range in her words: "the… solopreneur to… one of the largest black owned enterprises in the country." Size: not stated. TGS (The Gathering Spot, a private members' business and social club in Atlanta) has "about 5,000 members in Atlanta, and about a third are small business owners" (its CEO Ryan Wilson, on stage June 27, 2024). Subscriber list: "quarterly newsletter and event invitations" (Forum page).

**How topics and speakers are chosen.** A core group of about five meets regularly and asks its network "what content… they would like to see"; decisions on "an event, a speaker, a location" are made democratically and then bind everyone ("two feet in"). Panels lead with business owners, then "other service providers or other experts" (Dotson, 2024-07-17).

**Grade: B.** Sponsor-funded (June 2024: free to attendees through sponsors; Dotson: "right now we have our primary event sponsor," William Blair and Morris, Manning & Martin "came on as sponsors for an event"). Named outside keynote (Andrew Young, 2023), thanked with a foundation donation rather than a fee. No honorarium line. All American Speakers lists Young ("request booking information") but acts for buyers, so this is not an A (same rule as `alex-osborne-person.md`). Trend against: the March 2026 session was in-house.

**AI.** No AI session in the Forum. Aprio's AI webinars run in other tracks (pool notes).

**Next date.** Not posted on the Forum page or Aprio's events list (checked 2026-10-18). **INFERENCE (two signals): November 2026.** (1) November 6, 2024 and November 20, 2025 both ran; (2) Dotson in 2024: "we're in plans for our November event." Expansion stated: New York (done, Nov 2025) and Washington, D.C. (Epova, 2025-10-23).
