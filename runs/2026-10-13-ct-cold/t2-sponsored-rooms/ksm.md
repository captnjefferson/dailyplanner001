## 2. Room facts (T2)

**Series:** "Trucking Owners Business Roundtable" (Nashville, 2018) → "Trucking Owners & Leaders Roundtable" (Indianapolis, 2020 to 2024) → "Transportation Leaders Roundtable" (2025 on). Format: one day, "Presentation/Roundtable Discussion," sponsored dinner the night before. Up to 7.5 CPE credits. "Attendance is limited to industry participants." Antitrust guidelines are handed out (topics such as rates and wages are off limits).
