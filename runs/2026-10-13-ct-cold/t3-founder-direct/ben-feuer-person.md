## 2. Who he is

Ben Feuer is "a native of Philadelphia" who "grew up in a Reform household" (J. Weekly, 2015-12-11; 35 then, so about 46 now). Philosophy at Trinity College (Connecticut), then Northwestern Law (CALG bio). He meant to practice in Washington until a law-school girlfriend who had gone to art school in San Francisco talked him into visiting: "the only time in my life I've experienced love at first sight." He clerked for Ninth Circuit Judge Carlos Bea ("We're very close. We're good friends. We talk regularly"), then went solo in 2010, "four years out of law school with no clients whatsoever… I think I made $60,000 my first year." He has children ("before I had kids"; all 2026-09-17).

He built CALG against the billable-hour pyramid because "so many lawyers were unhappy because they had to work so hard": lawyers are paid a share of billings, work from anywhere (since 2012), and nobody bills 2,000 hours. "I don't have enough of an ego that I need my name on the door." He calls himself "a more extroverted type."

Faith and community are on his record: he founded the Jewish Bar Association of San Francisco in 2015 ("But there was no Jewish bar association. That was crazy," J. Weekly) and still sits on its board (https://www.jbasf.org/, fetched 2026-10-20). He co-chaired the Bar Association of San Francisco's Appellate Section 2017–2020 and won the Minority Bar Council's Unity Award for diversity work (CALG bio). Political giving on file: $1,025 to John Edwards for President, 2007 (FEC).
