## 2. Who he is

Rob Levin was a "'Big 6' CPA, CFO, COO, and CEO" before founding The New York Enterprise Report, a magazine and events business for New York owners, in 2003. He sold it after about ten years: "It was not a successful company, even though it looked wildly successful on the exterior… especially for 10 years while I'm raising a family in New York City." His diagnosis: "I was too deep in the day-to-day to act on what I already knew."

WBN came from his own life. An assistant he hired in 2013 "changed my business and changed my life," but she was employed by a company in El Salvador, "and I didn't like the way they treated her… I just said, 'I can do this better.'" The mission, in his words: "help awesome talent and awesome companies fulfill their dreams."

He is open about inner work: "Over the past ten or so years, that has meant better sleep, consistent exercise, watching what I eat and drink, meditation, and therapy." After NYER he found a limiting belief "about what I believed I deserved." He lives "in the Bronx, New York, with his family and their Goldendoodle, Charlie," is "a lifelong guitarist and concert enthusiast" who has "seen over a thousand shows and produced events featuring legends like Bob Dylan and The Red Hot Chili Peppers," and taught his kids to save weekly. Faith: not found.
