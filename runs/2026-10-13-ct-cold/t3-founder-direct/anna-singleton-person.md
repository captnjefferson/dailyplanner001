## 2. Who she is

Anna Singleton started Show My Property with her sister Christina in 2010. The first site sold a single idea: an apartment video with "a TV Host acting as a friendly guide is so effective at selling," because "a potential resident does not want to click through ten badly lit photos of their new home" (Wayback, 2012). Sixteen years later the agency shoots video, photography, renderings and social campaigns for apartment owners nationally, from an office on Victory Boulevard in Los Angeles.

The sisters run it together, and she says so every time she is quoted: "Christina and I are honored to lead a team that continually pushes the boundaries of creativity" (2023); "Christina and I are immensely proud of the collaborative culture we've fostered" (2024). The company calls itself "an approachable and inclusive family of content creators" (what-we-do page) and, on X, "small but MIGHTY." Its own share line for the 2023 release reads "Women-Owned Marketing Agency Lands a Spot on the Inc. 5000."

INFERENCE: Christina is the company's public-facing contact. Two signals: she is the named media contact on both releases (2023, 2024), and no personal channel for Anna was found anywhere.

On AI, the only first-person sentence on record is about weight, not doubt about the tools: "It's like a new full-time job on top of all the jobs we already have" (Inc., 2026-08-14). The company now sells an AI leasing assistant (§7).
