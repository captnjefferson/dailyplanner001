# T3 founder-direct candidates: founders who said in public that they are unsure about AI

- **Run date:** 2026-10-13, about 15:04 to 16:05 ET. Stopped early because the shared web-search budget for this turn ran out.
- **Window searched:** statements dated 2026-04-13 to 2026-10-13.
- **Result:** 5 people listed, against a target of 20.
  - 2 meet every criterion: William Vanderbloemen and Joel Gascoigne. Joel is a moderate fit; see his entry.
  - 3 are borderline, each with the reason stated: Paul Downs (size), Rob Levin (role and AI exposure) and David Heinemeier Hansson (role and fame).
  - 9 near-misses are listed at the end so nobody re-checks them.
- **Method:** read-only. WebSearch, WebFetch and curl only. No contact with anyone, no logins, no browser tools, and no LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram or X profile pages.
- **Lanes:** three lanes ran in parallel:
  1. founder-written newsletters, blogs, Substack and Medium (this agent);
