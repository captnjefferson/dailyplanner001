## 2. Who he is

37signals started as a web design firm; Basecamp was built "out of desperate necessity… Without it, we were embarrassing ourselves," and within about a year it out-earned the design work. He prizes independence: "One of the great privileges of owning an independent company is that you get to try all sorts of stuff no one else would ever give you permission to do" (2025-08-21). He distrusts certainty: "I don't think you're ever going to know if it's the right decision. You're just going to make a decision and then you're going to play it out" (2025-05-14). He is candid about the years they chased size: "I think there's probably some ego involved in it… maybe it's insecurity" (same episode).

Family and home show up plainly. "My wife and I" share the housework with a weekly housekeeper (2026-08-19); his wedding, he said, was "12 people in our backyard" (2026-07-22); his "folks are in town visiting us for a couple months" (2026-01-05). He has rowed on a Concept2 for "years" and calls it "a pinnacle product" (2025-11-18). He collects old Porsches and admits "deep regret right after winning a car" at auction (2026-07-22). For community, he republished a friend's letter after her home burned in Malibu's Franklin Fire: "If you know anyone who can reach Governor Newsom… please do" (2025-02-07). Faith: not found.
