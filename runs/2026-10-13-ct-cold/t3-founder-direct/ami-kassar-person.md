## 2. Who he is

Before MultiFunding, Kassar spent ten years at Advanta, a credit-card issuer to small-business owners, as chief innovation officer, where he founded Ideablob, an online community for owners. Brandeis BA, USC MBA. He "lives in the suburbs of Philadelphia with his wife, two children and his dog" (meetami). His daughter Charlotte launched a gifting company at 24; on his show he "opens up about trying not to be the overbearing entrepreneur dad" (episode 235, 2026-10-13).

The value he repeats is the golden rule: "treat every entrepreneur the way we would want to be treated" (team page). In June he drove to the airport himself for a Philippines-based teammate who had flown 26 hours to the offsite: "I've always believed that same principle applies to our employees" (2026-06-18).

He builds rooms. He founded EO Grit, "1500 members around the world online," and organized the first myEO Grit Unconference: about 60 founders in treehouses in Banning Mills, Georgia, no agenda, spotty cell service. "I don't often get emotional," he said of its last session; "I was a little teary eyed" (episode 233, 2026-09-15).

On AI he is a daily user who distrusts answers that look good: "garbage wrapped in a bow" (April 2026); "permission shopping faster" (2026-10-08). On record, he put "the same medical question" to ChatGPT and Claude and used the gap to sharpen his questions for his doctor (2026-07-30).
