## 2. Who he is

Joel Gascoigne was "born and raised in Sheffield, England" and lived in Utsunomiya, Japan from four to seven; this summer he is relearning Japanese ("I just hit level 4 after about 9 weeks of daily study," Bluesky, 2026-07-16). He launched Buffer in 2010 during a Hacker News "November Startup Sprint," and calls himself "an idealist at heart." His wife Jess opened her café, Creature Comforts, in Boulder in July 2023, "a decades-long dream"; their son Milo was born in May 2021. He "Moved to Chicago with the family this summer" (2026-09-17).

He has written openly about health: "In mid-2017, I hit burnout in a really big way and wound up taking a 6-week break," after his co-founder and CTO left and he and Jess went through "challenges in our relationship" (2019). He now reads David Whyte and Richard Schwartz's *No Bad Parts*, runs a four-day week, and says early intense focus "created an imbalanced life."

On the business, generosity is the core: "if Buffer was acquired by a Private Equity firm (don't worry, that won't happen), how many immediate changes would they make to extract more revenue? The longer that list, the more differentiated we are" (2026-06-12). He spent "literally ~8 years around that $20M number," fell to $18M, and crossed $25M in May 2026. On AI he is hands-on: "I now see it as a key part of my role to be tinkering with AI… Even with a technical background I constantly feel behind" (2026-01-22).
