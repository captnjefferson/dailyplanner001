## 2. Who he is

Jonathan Merel grew up around criminal law: "My father was a criminal defense attorney, and my uncle was a lawyer… we had phones ringing all night from the jail." He clerked at a family-law firm in his first year at Chicago-Kent and "It just clicked… I love interacting with people. I'm social."

He spent about seven years as an associate, "underappreciated… outbilling every other associate by hundreds of hours a year," and left in March 2009, "during a horrible recession. My daughter was about to be born." He took his law clerk, Melissa Caballero Dunn ("my Jerry Maguire moment"), now the managing partner. In July 2025 he said his kids are "teenagers in high school."

What drives him, in his words: "Fear of failure motivates me. It makes me get up and start working at five in the morning." He gives his cell number to every client and admits "I should set more boundaries." He tells every employee to see a therapist, and built a 25,000-square-foot office with meditation and Peloton rooms, a bar and a studio so people want to come in. "Seeing the success of the people who work for me is more fulfilling than my own success." He golfs, with his friend Ben Cohen as his tournament partner. No faith statement found. Community on record: the firm's $5,000 scholarship for future family lawyers, and an office he opens for "charity events, board meetings."
