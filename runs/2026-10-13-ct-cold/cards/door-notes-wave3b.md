# Door notes, wave 3b · drafted 2026-10-20

**The shape.** One thing noticed about their show, room or publication; one clause on who you are and why you're writing; then the give, either a topic for their audience with a line on why it fits, or two questions and an offer to share back. No credentials, no ask for a call, no links, under 120 words. The target the door reaches is never named.
**None sent.** Read or listen to each cited source yourself before sending. Unlike wave 3, these four carry the reviewed "what's still unsolved" clause alone, without the "six small businesses" line (the lead's instruction, 10/20). Every address below is the person's own, printed on a public page.

**Orgs, once.** Delaware Business Times (DBT): Delaware's statewide business paper; its readers are the state's owners and executives. Peter Levitan & Co.: Levitan's practice advising advertising agencies on winning new business; he also blogs for agency owners. 21 Hats: Loren Feldman's weekly podcast and newsletter in which a rotating panel of small-business owners talks through real problems; "Dashboard" is its one-on-one interview episode and "Brainstorm" its listener-challenge episode. Grappling with the Gray: Yonason Goldson's weekly ethics panel podcast, three rotating guests on one dilemma.
