It hosts owner events as business development. **Manufacturing Education Day** runs in three Ohio cities and is programmed by Brickwood as the manufacturing practice leader. ## 3. Does the small-business toolkit exist

Short answer: methods yes, processes yes for a cohort or a workshop, builds partly. The missing build is the one that matters after the first booking.

### Methods, and they are the distinctive part

- The five axes from the April 2025 cohort (founder mindset trust-and-control against experimentation; personalization against standardization; emotional friction against technical; exploration and piloting; standardization and scaling), each with cross-cohort patterns and consulting implications. Six small services organizations, five group calls plus 1:1s. The finding nobody else in these rooms is saying: friction was as often emotional as technical, and naming it is what unlocked progress.
- The IHA S2 arc built this month is the owner-and-team talk in hospital clothes. Chapters: people first; watch the work change; guardrails you already run; from one win to capability. S1 supplies the fear nobody says out loud, why rollouts fail, how new people learn, and "four moves for Monday." S2 closes with questions for the executive team and board, which for an owner becomes questions for you and your two lieutenants. Fact-checked line by line on 10/1. Re-skin for owners, do not rebuild.
- SPARK, the before-and-after device, the responsible-use list and the hallucination checklist are evergreen and already portable.

### Processes, real and one-off

- Kickoff AI assessment form, group calls plus 1:1s, facilitator guides with timeboxes, agenda and meeting-overview prompts, per-client roadmaps with timeframes, and the Discovery to Intensive to Support container from the 2024 program prospectus.
- None of this is packaged. Each run was assembled for that client.

### Builds, bespoke and a year old

- Innovo: Claude for document analysis, an MCP bridge to Teams document storage, environmental-scan automation with QC checkpoints. Sales Done Differently: transcript-to-deliverable pipeline on the Claude and ChatGPT APIs, a content-calendar system, Zapier admin automations, an AI style guide. Jigsaw: AI analytics dashboards. REV: a 64,000-character prompting guide. Time Away and Minds Unstuck: templates and checklists. PromptLab v3 for MCIE in May 2026.
- The materials assessment done for IHA found that every use case in the corpus was individual-task prompting and that no organizational-change curriculum existed; Chapter 0 was a prospectus. The IHA decks closed that gap in deck form. Nothing closes it in product form.
- Every quantitative outcome from the cohort is projected, not measured. There is no number a founder in one of these rooms can repeat.

### The gap, stated plainly

There is no named thing an owner buys after the talk. When an owner in Garrido's group asks what working with Jefferson looks like, the answer has to exist with a shape, a duration, and a price. That is the one build to settle before the first slot lands, and it is better that it surfaced now than after.

## 5. Approach

The scaffold approved on 10/14, with the formula worry handled by variety and by what the second touch is.

1. **One genuine question per person, or no note.** The question comes from what they wrote. Garrido, Luther and Brickwood each just ran an AI session for owners, so "what happened after" is a real question and their answers are comparable. Chediak's and Mangano's questions are about their rooms. Flanagan's and Winters's exist only if Jefferson actually wants the answer.
2. **Channel and moment differ by person.** Per the table. Nothing goes out on the same day, and two of the five Tier 1 and 2 notes are date-gated (Brickwood after Oct 30, Mangano after Nov 12).
3. **The share-back is the second touch and a promise.** With three comparable answers it becomes a short written pattern: what three people who run owner rooms saw after their AI session. That piece is also the research for the owner version of the talk. It is the credential, and it never pitches.
4. **Convening is the third move, only after share-backs.** A 45-minute call with two or three of them on the owner-and-team problem. They watch Jefferson run a room. That is the closest thing this business has to a demo.
5. **Nobody is asked for the slot.** It comes when they raise it or when the calendar does: Brickwood's 2027 theme, Chediak's June, the EO chapter year. If it never comes from a given person, that person stays a relationship and the list was still right.
6. **Cadence is slow and the count is small.** Ten a thread is no longer the number. The LinkedIn watch review date stays 2026-11-15.
