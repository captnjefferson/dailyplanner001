Thank you Rasika Kalamegham for organizing and moderating an amazing panel "Crisis Can Be Opportunity: FDA Reform for the Good of Patients" at BIO2026. It was an honor to share the stage with Jeff Allen, Lowell Schiller, Eva Temkin, and Annetta Beauregard. We didn't shy away from the disruption: leadership transitions, workforce strain, real uncertainty across the ecosystem. But disruption and opportunity aren't opposites. What gives me hope is that the people most committed to getting medicines to patients faster are still in the room and still finding common ground. We talked about AI and technology, RWE, and the need for greater predictability and consistency in regulatory decision-making, plus a return to open, transparent policy and rulemaking. But one thing was obvious to everyone: none of it happens unless FDA's career staff are supported.
