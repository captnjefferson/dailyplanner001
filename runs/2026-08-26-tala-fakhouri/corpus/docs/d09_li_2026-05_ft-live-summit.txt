Wonderful to be in conversation with Fiona H. Marshall, Richard Bonneau, and moderator Michael Peel at the FT Live US Pharma and Biotech Summit. AI in drug development is not hype (there is a lot of hype undoubtedly such as the claim that we will solve all diseases in five years). FDA has received over 1,000 submissions with AI components since 2016 and authorized more than 1,400 AI-enabled medical devices, with the first going back to 1995. The challenge is scale. That requires regulatory clarity on what valid looks like in an inspection, real collaboration between AI developers and domain experts, and a serious commitment to pre-competitive data sharing. No single sponsor has the data to build models that generalize. The field moves faster when we share.
